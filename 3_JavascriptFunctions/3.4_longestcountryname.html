Switzerland


Switzerland (official name Swiss Confederation) is a country in Europe bordering Italy, France, Germany, Austria, and Liechtenstein. It has a policy of armed neutrality and hasn't fought in a world war since 1815. The capital is Bern, and its largest city is Bern.


Landmarks

  • Chillon Castle
  • Rhine Falls
  • Chapel Bridge
