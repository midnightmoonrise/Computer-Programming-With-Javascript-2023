Fish Care

Because I lost so many tankmates, at least 10 guppies and 2 other cherry barbs, due to water quality issues, I have quite a bit to say on this.

It's important to maintain the fish tank. Get a good filter... and make sure you CHANGE the filter every month!! Also make sure to replace the water and control its pH and temperature.

Also make sure to put a bit of gravel, plants, and decorations into the tank.
