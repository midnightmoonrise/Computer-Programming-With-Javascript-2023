Eyes of My Pet

I am Siming’s pet fish. I think she’s a confused, overwhelmed and busy individual so I do not exactly blame her for not giving me or my tankmates names. Then again, the 3 of us look identical - we’re all tiny and orange. There was one point when we shared the tank with a fish with one eye and she was observant enough to notice it and was creative enough to name him One Eye. He’s dead now. I think I like her over her brother, as her brother once put a Thomas the Tank Engine sticker on our tank. I don’t see her much; she spends most of her time cooped up in her room halfway across the house. I presume she’s drawing because whenever I see her she’s always carrying some sort of sketchbook and colored pencils. Or practicing piano. One of the two, so she’s definitely pretty artistic. I overhear her tell her brother how much she hates math though…
